Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

SasTown Hub ("we", "us") runs a free community and business directory for Sasolburg and Zamdela, available on this website and the SasTown Hub Android app. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who is responsible for your information

Kabelo Moloi, trading as AethelSt8, is the responsible party for SasTown Hub. You can reach us at kmoloi928@gmail.com or WhatsApp +27 78 728 0069.

What we collect

SasTown Hub has no public user accounts, logins, or tracking cookies for visitors browsing the directory. The only personal information we collect is what you choose to submit through the "Add My Business" form, whether on the website or in the app:

  • Business or talent name
  • Category
  • Description of your business, service, or talent
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number (optional)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Facebook and/or Instagram links (optional)
  • General location (Sasolburg or Zamdela) and, optionally, a description of where you're based
  • A logo or photo you upload

We don't ask for ID numbers, banking details, or any other sensitive personal information, and you should not include any in your submission.

Why we collect it, and what we do with it

We use this information for one purpose: to publish it as a public listing on the SasTown Hub directory, so people in Sasolburg and Zamdela can find and contact you. Every submission is reviewed by us before it goes live - it isn't published automatically. We don't sell, rent, or share your information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Submissions are stored in a database hosted on secured cloud infrastructure, and your information isn't transferred to any third party for purposes unrelated to operating the directory.

How long we keep it, and account/data deletion

We keep your listing information for as long as it remains active on the directory. Since SasTown Hub doesn't use public user accounts, there's no in-app "delete account" button - instead, if you'd like your listing removed or any information you've submitted deleted at any time, contact us using the details above (email or WhatsApp) and we'll action your request promptly, typically within a few days.

The SasTown Hub app specifically

The Android app doesn't use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools, doesn't access your contacts or precise location, and doesn't create any account for you. It caches directory listings on your device so you can browse offline, and if you submit a business while you're offline, the submission (including the photo you chose) is saved on your device and sent automatically once you're back online - it isn't shared anywhere else in the meantime.

Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that's wrong or out of date
  • Ask us to delete your information or remove your listing
  • Object to how we're using your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe we haven't handled your information properly

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep your information safe, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic and restricting access to the admin review tools to a single authenticated administrator.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as SasTown Hub grows. Significant changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
